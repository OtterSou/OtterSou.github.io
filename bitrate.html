Syntax

  • ( )
  • binary operator: + - * /
  • unary operator: + -
  • number: integer or decimal
  • time unit: h m s
    • can be concatenated e.g. 1m30s = 90s
  • prefix: k ki K Ki M Mi G Gi T Ti
    • 1000n without i, 1024n with i, except K is 1024
  • information unit: b B
  • bitrate: information unit followed by ps
  • bare units have a value of 1 e.g. 1kb/s = 1kb/1s = 1kbps
